Listening to 1,133 stars for the signals nobody else looked for.

Every star Breakthrough Listen pointed the Green Bank Telescope at between 2016 and 2019, re-searched for wide, curving, flickering and comb-shaped signals. Everything we flag goes down the same five-rung ladder in public. Below: the sky we're listening to, the radio band we're listening in, and what the run looks like while it's going.

The sky we're listening to

Every dot is a real star from the sample, at its real position. The glowing band is the Milky Way. Press play to replay three years of observing in thirty seconds, in the order the telescope visited them.

The radio band we're listening in

1.1 to 2.8 GHz. Amber bands are known human transmitters, striped bands are filters where the receiver records nothing, and blue is nature's own quiet zone, the "water hole". Pins are today's 24 flagged signals, colored by how they were eliminated.
